# Product

## Register

product

## Users

An independent creative professional uses Earnline to record income, review current work, and keep a personal ledger in sync across their own devices. They return to the app frequently, usually in short focused moments, and need controls to be immediately understandable without turning the ledger into an administration tool.

## Product Purpose

Earnline is a personal income ledger. Its interface should make recording work and reviewing earnings feel direct, calm, and trustworthy. Settings should expose everyday preferences while keeping operational configuration out of the normal path.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, contemporary.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic colored settings glyphs, dense developer dashboards, tiny floating controls, and unexplained toolbar actions. Do not make a personal finance ledger feel like an enterprise admin console.

## Design Principles

1. Put everyday actions within immediate reach.
2. Let advanced controls recede until intentionally requested.
3. Use one coherent, monochrome icon language for utility actions.
4. Prefer familiar iOS behavior over decorative novelty.
5. Preserve readable, accessible interaction targets at every Dynamic Type size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, high-contrast legibility, and 44-point minimum touch targets. Respect the app's existing Reduce Motion behavior.
